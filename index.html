About

Hi, I'm Mary! I'm a PhD student at MIT studying Aerospace Engineering, with a focus on satellites and space systems. I successfully defended my dissertation on November 17th, and am now looking for full time positions. I started my aerospace career initially focusing on structural design and test engineering, later pivoted to autonomy, and finally ended up in satellite systems engineering and mission design. I have found my intersection of interests in these areas makes me a well-rounded engineer with a holistic understanding of complex systems. These backgrounds have culminated in my PhD thesis on designing a conceptual mizssion for a satellite that can autonomously identify targets of interest and plan its observations. My targets of interest are clouds! I study different system architectures for this project through a custom simulation environment I built in Python.

I have experience with project management, mission design, systems engineering, remote sensing, autonomous systems, and structural design. I value strong technical communication and teamwork in engineering work. My goal is to leverage my experience and skills to contribute to innovative projects that make a positive impact on society and the environment. A more resilient future is possible through unending dedication to our planet.

I'm currently looking for career opportunities in the Greater Boston area, particularly in satellite systems engineering for Earth observation and remote sensing applications. Email me at marydahl@mit.edu to connect!

My PhD project! A conceptual satellite to autonomously target and observe clouds.

X
